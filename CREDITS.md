# Photo credits

All venue and hero photographs in this project are real photographs from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), used under the licences listed below (CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA). No AI-generated images are used.

**Modifications:** every photo was cropped (to 3:2, or a wider banner crop for the home hero), resized to at most 1600 px wide and re-compressed as JPEG. Nothing else was changed. Where a licence requires ShareAlike (CC BY-SA), the adapted files remain under that same licence.

**The venues are fictional demo data.** The photos show real places around the world and are only used as atmosphere, so no real business, venue or person is implied.

File names are the demo venue slug plus a number (`<slug>-1.jpg` ...) under `uploads/venues/seed/`, or `hero.jpg` / `auth.jpg` in the player app's `public/img/`.

There are 59 photos.
